    Benvenuti · Welcome to the family

    Charming, contemporary — and always a good time.

    i.

    Unique

    Fresh Italian dishes you're bound to love and crave, served in an energetic, upscale casual room with contemporary décor.

    ii.

    Inviting

    Handcrafted woodwork, Tuscan colours and hand-painted murals. You'll be greeted by our “host with the most,” Dale “Daleo” Kirshnitz, and familiar faces from the Hartford restaurant scene.

    iii.

    Exciting

    A creative wine list with an extensive by-the-glass selection, specialty martinis and fun cocktails at our lively bar. Bring your appetite, your friends and your family.

    Owned and run by Jimmy Cosgrove and Andy Rizzo. Our executive chef and a highly motivated culinary team cook every dish with real passion.
    La Cucina

    Our menus

    Everything is right here, so there's nothing to download. Gluten-free pasta, spaghetti squash or gnocchi can be swapped in, and we'll gladly accommodate other vegetarian requests.

    Bar & Lounge

    A great meeting place

    A vast selection of fine wines, cordials, liquor and beers on tap, with the full menu served at the bar. Our espresso martinis are made to order and available to go.
